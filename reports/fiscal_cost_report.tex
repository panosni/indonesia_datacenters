\author{}

\subsection*{Overview}
Indonesia's data centre market has attracted IDR~385.8~trillion (USD~24.4~billion) in identified investment across 49~facilities, encompassing 3475~MW of capacity (912~MW operational, 2563~MW pipeline). Three fiscal instruments apply: a CIT holiday of up to 20~years (PMK-130/2020); a post-holiday investment allowance of 30\% of capex under GR~No.~45/2019; and VAT plus import duty exemptions for Batam Nongsa Digital Park SEZ operators. Total cumulative fiscal cost is estimated at \textbf{IDR~130.659~trillion (USD~8.27~billion)}, equivalent to \textbf{0.590\%~of 2024 GDP} and 19.5\% of the annual government education budget.
